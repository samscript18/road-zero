# Road Zero web-brand asset provenance

These are 2D website identity/preview images, not game geometry or imported 3D meshes. Both were generated for this project using the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-25. No downloaded or third-party artwork was used.

## Logo

Source: generated transparent 1254 × 1254 PNG. Web derivatives: `public/road-zero-logo.png` (512 × 512), `public/apple-touch-icon.png` (180 × 180), and `public/favicon.png` (64 × 64). The resize-only derivatives preserve the generated alpha channel.

Final prompt: “Create an original compact emblem for ROAD ZERO, an analog 1982 hillside motorsport racing game. A single warm vermilion vintage compact racing coupe in dynamic three-quarter view hugging a cream-colored hairpin bend; the bend subtly forms a zero. Strong simple silhouette that reads at small icon sizes. Screen-printed vintage motorsport badge, crisp flat vector-like shapes, transparent background, no text, no trademarks, no neon or sci-fi.”

## Social/SEO preview

Source: generated wide 1730 × 909 illustration, then an image-generation edit adding the title on the dark left side. Web derivative: `public/road-zero-og.jpg` (1200 × 630, JPEG quality 84).

Base prompt: “A warm analog hill-climb race: a rounded compact vermilion racing coupe in the foreground rounding a dusty hairpin, three smaller distinct ochre, blue, and olive rival cars further along the road, timber barriers, cloth pennants, a small human crowd, orchard trees, and layered mountains fading into golden late-afternoon haze. Premium screen-printed vintage motorsport poster meets stylized miniature-world illustration. Landscape social card, racing action on the right, calm dark negative space on the left for a title. No text, trademarks, watermark, neon, or sci-fi.”

Final edit prompt: “Add clean, professionally typeset text only in the dark negative space on the left. Large two-line title ‘ROAD’ then ‘ZERO’; small line below ‘THE CANTERA HILL-CLIMB · 1982’. Bold condensed vintage motorsport lettering, cream title with ZERO in warm vermilion. Preserve the cars, road, crowd, mountains, colors, and illumination.”

The final images were visually checked for title spelling, composition, and format. The social preview uses the current Vercel site URL in metadata; update that URL if the canonical public website changes.
