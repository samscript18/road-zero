# Track composition pass

This plan treats each circuit as a sequence of authored shots. Percentages refer to normalized route progress and are placement guides, not new gameplay checkpoints.

## Orchard Sprint

Identity: warm, flowing, agricultural and welcoming.

| section | progress | authored composition |
|---|---:|---|
| start/finish | 0–8% | Timber gantry, marshal hut, cream/orange flag fan, stacked hay and a compact crowd bank. Background village peeks through rather than sitting directly behind the road. |
| hero corner | 18–30% | Outside-canopy spectator pocket above a timber fence; orchard crowns overlap behind it; close grass and bale rhythm establishes parallax. Signature silhouette: one broad stone pine paired with the low canopy. |
| breathing section | 31–45% | Open dry-grass hillside and framed mountain vista; sparse fencing preserves speed/readability. |
| overtaking zone | 46–64% | Long edge line, alternating fence openings and orchard rows; spectators cluster only at braking end. |
| dense festival pocket | 65–82% | Paddock shade, support silhouettes, bunting, crates and two crowd terraces placed on the outside of the bend. |
| finish approach | 83–100% | Increasing flags/hay/crowd rhythm funnels the eye to the gantry. |

Distance bands: grass/posts/bales at 4.5–8 m; trees/canopies/crowds at 9–28 m; sage slopes, village and cool mountain layers beyond 80 m.

## Quarry Loop

Identity: exposed stone, precision braking, darker ground and vertical cuts.

| section | progress | authored composition |
|---|---:|---|
| start/finish | 0–8% | Smaller gantry against a stepped rock face, timing hut above road level, restrained crowd. |
| technical hero corner | 16–32% | Warm cut-rock wall on outside, cream apex blocks and high marshal ledge. Landmark: twin quarry faces framing a narrow view forward. |
| breathing section | 33–46% | Sparse tough vegetation, dusty shoulder and a deep view into the worked basin. |
| Technician zone | 47–68% | Repeating but irregular stone wall cadence makes braking distance legible; orange/cream boards mark turn-in without text. |
| elevated spectator zone | 69–84% | Crowd silhouettes and canopy on a rock terrace, safely above the racing surface. |
| finish approach | 85–100% | Stone walls step down and reveal the gantry; dust and flags remain concentrated at the final braking point. |

Distance bands: gravel/rocks/walls near road; cut faces, hut and elevated crowd midground; cooler quarry rims and distant peaks behind.

## Summit Run

Identity: exposed, prestigious and climactic, with selective final-event density.

| section | progress | authored composition |
|---|---:|---|
| start/finish | 0–10% | Largest flag array, denser crowd terraces and a prestigious cream/orange gantry against open sky. |
| exposed climb | 11–30% | Sparse foreground posts and rock, strong layered mountain vista; intentionally quiet. |
| hero switchback | 31–52% | Stone retaining wall below, spectator ledge above, tall cypress/flag landmark; maximum vertical layering. |
| breathing ridge | 53–68% | Minimal furniture, cooler haze and distant overlapping ridges emphasize altitude. |
| championship spectator zone | 69–86% | Two asymmetric crowd/canopy pockets, bunting and marshal platform around the Defender's strongest section. |
| final approach | 87–100% | Increasing crowd, flags and warm cloth accents; strongest finish composition and celebration effects. |

Distance bands: close stone/posts/grass; ledges, crowds and structures in midground; at least three progressively cooler mountain layers in background.

## Placement rules

- Density follows race drama, never a uniform interval.
- Repeated objects appear in 2–5 item clusters with gaps, not grids.
- Outside-of-corner landmarks frame the road; apex furniture remains low.
- Background forms never intersect the road silhouette or hide corner direction.
- Crowd treatment stays beyond close inspection distance and is instanced/batched by material.
