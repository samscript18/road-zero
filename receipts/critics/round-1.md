# Critic round 1 — composition

## Decision

**FAIL**

## Single largest reason

The current racing composition has no authored, inhabited midground or layered background, so it reads as a car moving across a sparse procedural test field rather than an analog hillside motorsport festival.

## Evidence

- In `desktop-racing.png`, most of the frame is divided into three broad, flat bands: dark road, uninterrupted brown terrain and pale sky. The few evenly distributed spherical trees and isolated faceted rocks do not establish a place, a corner identity or meaningful depth.
- The target frames use the road as the spine of a dense depth stack: close barriers/grass/stone in the foreground, spectators and track furniture tied to the corner in the midground, then overlapping hills, village silhouettes and atmospheric terrain behind them. The current frame has essentially no comparable midground and its horizon is empty.
- The current props look scattered beside the track rather than composed in response to it. They neither frame the next bend nor create an asymmetric landmark, failing visual claims 5, 8 and 14.
- `mobile-racing.png` makes the issue more severe: nearly half the view becomes undifferentiated brown ground, with the player car isolated against it and the road pushed to one side. The frame communicates off-road void more strongly than hill-climb racing.
- The menu typography and cream/charcoal treatment are directionally coherent, but the real-time backdrop behind it exposes the same flat, unfinished world and therefore cannot carry the intended premium festival identity.

## Highest-impact fix

Rebuild one complete hero corner as an authored three-layer composition before adding more distributed props: use a close physical road edge (timber barrier, hay and dry grass) to frame the foreground; place one asymmetric spectator-canopy/marshal cluster on the outside of the bend as the midground landmark; and replace the empty horizon with two or three overlapping mountain/settlement silhouettes under atmospheric haze. Recompose the mobile chase camera for that corner so road direction occupies the centre of the view instead of revealing a large empty terrain field.

This single intervention should be judged again in motion before expanding the scenery kit elsewhere.
