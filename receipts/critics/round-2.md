# Critic round 2 — vehicles and materials

## Verdict: FAIL

### Single largest issue

The player car does not preserve the reference coupe's defining fastback silhouette and believable body-over-wheel stance. In the candidate sheet, A and C turn the body into a short, bulbous toy with an almost circular roof pod, while B is visibly stacked planar boxes. In the moving desktop and mobile frames, the integrated rear view therefore collapses into a tiny red blob: the greenhouse, rear deck, wheel arches, tyres, and shoulder line cannot be separated, and the paint/glass division is reduced to a black cap. This fails the vehicle-form and hero-recognition claims before finer material quality can meaningfully be judged. The distant rivals are similarly too small to demonstrate silhouette-based distinction.

### Highest-impact fix

Rebuild the selected player shell around one continuous low fastback profile: lengthen and lower the greenhouse, carry a clear roof-to-rear-deck transition, cut visibly separate wheel openings around larger exposed tyres, and retain distinct rear shoulders and glass. Then frame that revised silhouette at the locked 30–38% landscape height in the chase camera. Do not polish the current bulb/roof-pod form; its proportions are the structural problem.

The reference image, candidate verification sheet, and both moving screenshots were judged directly. No other critic output was inspected.
