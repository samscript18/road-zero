| A | overlapping rounded masses and sculpted wheel brows | 9,224 | 1.725 × 1.365 × 3.962 | Selected: clearest compact-coupe silhouette, strongest fender/body relationship, most attractive chase view. |

All candidates are grounded at y=0, face +Z, expose four wheel pivots through `userData.parts`, and retain hierarchy for steering/rotation.
