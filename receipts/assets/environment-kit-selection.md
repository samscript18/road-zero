# Environment kit selection

Official verifier: 3/3 clean.

| Candidate | Strategy | Triangles | Measured size (m) | Visual decision |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | rounded primitive/mass family | 2,108 | 23.010 × 5.923 × 7.479 | Rejected: coherent but several props read softly at race distance. |
| B | extruded profiles and lathed forms | 3,984 | 21.121 × 5.655 × 8.143 | Selected: strongest gabled village, cloth finish arch, canopy silhouette, and timber/plaster separation. |
| C | layered frame/panel family | 1,570 | 23.902 × 5.805 × 7.550 | Rejected: efficient and crisp but visually lighter and less substantial than B. |

Each independent candidate contains marshal hut, timber barrier, hay bale, spectator canopy, roadside tree, rock formation, paddock structure, village module, and finish landmark. The selected hierarchy is cloned directly into track-specific placements; it is not baked or flattened.
